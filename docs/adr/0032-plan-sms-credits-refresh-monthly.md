# Plan SMS Credits refresh monthly; purchased SMS Credits do not expire

Plan SMS Credits from a Salon's SMS Allowance last for one monthly allowance period. Unused plan credits expire at the period boundary and are replaced by a fresh allowance only while the Salon Subscription remains active; they never roll over.

An annual Salon Subscription uses twelve monthly allowance periods rather than granting the entire year's plan SMS Credits upfront.

SMS Credits bought separately through an SMS Credit Bundle use a distinct purchased balance and never expire. The SMS Credit ledger preserves each credit source, and sending consumes plan credits before purchased credits so the expiring balance is used first.

SMS Credit Grants join the same non-expiring balance as purchased SMS Credits. Their ledger entries retain `admin_grant` as the source; no third wallet balance is introduced.
