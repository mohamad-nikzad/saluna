# Active Staff Profiles are the Salon Subscription metric

Public Salon Subscription tiers scale by the maximum number of active Staff Profiles. Login identities, Staff Profile Access links, Appointments, Clients, and salon revenue do not affect the base subscription price. Existing core product features remain available across paid tiers; direct variable-cost services such as SMS may be metered separately.

An active Staff Profile represents recurring scheduling and operational value whether or not a login identity has accepted Staff Profile Access. This preserves ADR-0006's separation between the salon-owned operational profile and a person's identity, avoids encouraging shared logins, and gives salons a predictable price that does not penalize increased appointment or client activity.
