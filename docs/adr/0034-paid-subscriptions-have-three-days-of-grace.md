# Paid Salon Subscriptions have three days of Grace Period

When a paid Salon Subscription ends without renewal, the Salon retains normal operational access for a three-day Grace Period. It receives no new plan SMS Allowance during grace, but may use credits already available in its balances.

If no Verified Payment renews the Subscription before grace ends, the Salon becomes read-only. Trials receive no Grace Period and become read-only immediately at expiry. This gives paying salons time to recover from a missed manual renewal without extending free evaluation accounts.
