# Internal salons may have Unlimited Access

Saluna may mark its own Salon or a test Salon as having Unlimited Access. This is permanent commercial access: it requires no payment or Salon Subscription, has no active Staff Profile limit, has no expiry, and requires no recorded reason.

Unlimited Access is an explicit Salon entitlement rather than a hard-coded Salon ID or user-account exception. It keeps internal access independent of the Trial and paid subscription lifecycle while using the same product paths as other active Salons.

Provider-billed SMS still consumes SMS Credits. A Platform Owner may add an SMS Credit Grant to the Salon whenever needed; Unlimited Access itself does not create an unlimited SMS balance.
