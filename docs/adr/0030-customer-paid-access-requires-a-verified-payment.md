# Customer paid access requires a Verified Payment

A customer Salon receives paid access only after Saluna verifies the exact Subscription Purchase with the payment provider. A browser return or customer-supplied payment evidence is not proof of payment, and platform staff cannot manually mark a customer Subscription Purchase as paid.

Unlimited Access remains the explicit mechanism for Saluna's own or test Salons; it is not a paid purchase. Offline-payment activation is deferred until real gateway outages or sales losses demonstrate that the extra reconciliation path is necessary.
