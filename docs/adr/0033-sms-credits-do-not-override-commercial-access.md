# SMS Credits do not override commercial access

A Salon may send salon-funded SMS only during an active Trial, paid Salon Subscription, or Unlimited Access. Remaining purchased or admin-granted SMS Credits stay on a read-only Salon but cannot be used until commercial access returns.

This keeps read-only access non-operational while preserving non-expiring credits for a later renewal. Saluna-funded authentication, account recovery, and identity-verification SMS remain available independently because they do not consume Salon SMS Credits.
