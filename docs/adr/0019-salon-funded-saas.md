# Saluna is salon-funded SaaS

Saluna's paying customer is the salon. Revenue may come from software subscriptions, assisted setup, and metered communication services. Customer-facing features, including Appointment Requests, operate on behalf of the salon, which retains the relationship with each Client.

Saluna will not initially operate as a consumer marketplace, sell leads or advertising, or make transaction commissions its core revenue model. This keeps product identity, data ownership, onboarding, and distribution aligned around helping salons run and grow their own businesses. Accepting deposits or other payments later does not by itself change this model.
