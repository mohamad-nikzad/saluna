# Staff Profile Limits never deactivate Staff Profiles

Creating or reactivating a Staff Profile is blocked when it would exceed the Salon Subscription's Staff Profile Limit. A Salon cannot buy or activate a lower tier until its manager deliberately deactivates enough profiles to fit.

Saluna never automatically deactivates or deletes Staff Profiles for a downgrade or subscription expiry. Expiry makes the Salon read-only while preserving profiles, schedules, access links, Appointments, and history. This enforces the pricing metric without changing salon-owned operational records behind the manager's back.
