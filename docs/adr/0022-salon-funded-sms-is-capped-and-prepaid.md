# Salon-funded SMS is capped and prepaid

Each Salon Subscription includes a capped SMS Allowance. A Salon that needs additional salon-funded SMS buys an SMS Credit Bundle before sending. Saluna will not promise unlimited SMS or absorb unbounded customer-messaging costs inside the base subscription price.

Authentication, account recovery, and identity-verification SMS remain Saluna-funded and never consume the Salon's SMS Credits. This keeps access and security independent of a Salon's commercial messaging balance while making the variable cost of messages sent for salon operations explicit and sustainable.

Holding SMS Credits does not override commercial access. A read-only Salon keeps its non-expiring balance but cannot send salon-funded SMS until it renews or receives Unlimited Access.

A Platform Owner may add an explicit SMS Credit Grant without a purchase, including for an internal Salon. The grant adds finite, non-expiring credits to the same balance as purchased SMS Credits; it does not create unlimited SMS usage.

Allowance sizes and bundle prices remain launch parameters. Under ADR-0032, plan SMS Credits expire at each monthly allowance boundary, while separately purchased SMS Credits never expire.
