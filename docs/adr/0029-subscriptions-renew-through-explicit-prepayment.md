# Salon Subscriptions renew through explicit prepayment

A Salon Subscription covers a fixed prepaid term. Renewal occurs only when the Salon explicitly completes another Subscription Purchase. Saluna stores no card or payment mandate and does not initiate automatic charges.

Cancellation therefore means choosing not to renew. This model avoids building recurring-charge and stored-payment machinery before supported local payment rails and customer demand justify it; a future auto-renewal option would require a separate decision.
