# Subscription prices are guaranteed per paid term

Each Subscription Purchase preserves the price and limits sold for that paid term. Saluna does not change an active purchase when it publishes new pricing.

A renewal is a new Subscription Purchase using the currently published offer after advance notice. Saluna will not promise lifetime price grandfathering or maintain permanent legacy plans for previous purchasers. This protects an active agreement without making every historical price part of the future entitlement model.
