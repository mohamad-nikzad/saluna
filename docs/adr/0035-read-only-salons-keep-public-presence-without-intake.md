# Read-only Salons keep public presence without AppointmentRequest intake

A Salon with Read-Only Access keeps its public Salon page and contact information visible. The page does not accept new AppointmentRequests and explains that online requests are temporarily unavailable.

This preserves the Salon's established public link and lets Clients contact it directly without continuing Saluna's operational intake service for an unpaid Salon. Existing AppointmentRequests and Salon data remain readable and unchanged.
