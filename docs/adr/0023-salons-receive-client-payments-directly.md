# Salons receive Client Payments directly

The Salon is the merchant and beneficiary for every Client Payment. Saluna may facilitate a deposit or service-payment flow through a salon-owned payment account, but it will not receive, pool, hold, or later pay out those funds.

This boundary keeps the commercial relationship established by ADR-0019 intact and avoids making Saluna responsible for custody, salon settlement, or a marketplace payout ledger. Payments a Salon makes to Saluna for a Salon Subscription or SMS Credit Bundle remain separate Saluna sales and are not Client Payments.
