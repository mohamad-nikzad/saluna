# Each Salon has its own Salon Subscription

One Salon Subscription covers exactly one Salon. Its commercial state, active Staff Profile limit, SMS Allowance, and purchased or granted SMS Credits belong to that Salon and are not pooled with another Salon owned or operated by the same people.

This matches Saluna's existing Salon tenant and operational ownership boundary. Chain-wide contracts, organization billing, and shared allowances are deferred until real multi-salon demand justifies adding a commercial layer above individual Salons.
